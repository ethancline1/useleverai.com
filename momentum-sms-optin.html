How this consent is captured

Who opts in
The instructor themselves, logged into their own staff account.
Checkbox state
Unchecked by default — the instructor must actively select it. No pre-checking.
Message type
Transactional/operational only — a notification when a client books or cancels a private lesson with that instructor. No marketing.
Frequency
Varies with booking activity (roughly one message per booking event).
Opt-out
Reply STOP at any time, or uncheck the box in the same settings screen.
Brand
Momentum Martial Arts, operating on software built and operated by Lever AI LLC.
